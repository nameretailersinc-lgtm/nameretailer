SaaS guest post planning and measurement checklist

[ ] Name the developer, founder or buyer audience and the decision they face.
[ ] Review recent articles in the proposed publication section.
[ ] Request dated audience evidence; do not treat DR as readership.
[ ] Pitch a useful problem, method and evidence you can supply.
[ ] Confirm placement, writing, editing and revision costs in the quote.
[ ] Confirm sponsorship label, paid-link qualification and publication schedule.
[ ] Agree a destination that continues the reader's task.
[ ] Confirm tracking parameters are acceptable to the publisher.

Suggested campaign URL structure (replace placeholders; omit personal data):
utm_source=[publisher_slug]&utm_medium=sponsored_content&utm_campaign=[campaign_slug]&utm_content=[article_slug]

[ ] Test referral attribution and the sign-up, trial and demo-request events.
[ ] Record the final URL, publication date and approved article version.
[ ] Choose a measurement window appropriate to the sales cycle.
[ ] Record actual referral visits and actions; mark missing attribution unknown.
[ ] Note overlapping campaigns and destination changes before interpreting results.
[ ] Keep observed results separate from supplied domain metrics and SEO claims.

This worksheet contains no publisher commitments or assumed campaign outcomes.
