The pasted list does not establish that Google currently shows these as related searches.

The guest-post directory answers how to find a marketplace, how to choose one and how to compare publication websites. Its existing niche directories, price bands, profiles and guides supply the next actions, so these answers do not need three repetitive new pages.

## Measurement after deployment

Confirm the report's provider, market and competitor inputs before assigning volume or difficulty. In Search Console, group relevant queries and compare page/query clicks, impressions and position over equivalent periods, filtered by country and device. Record deployment dates and assess whether the mapped pages satisfy their intended queries. Use consented analytics to inspect publication views and placement-plan actions, without treating query suggestions as measured demand.

Google's [people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content) supports useful, accurate content for an intended audience. [Search Console's Search performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en) supplies site query and page performance; it does not substantiate an unspecified competitor keyword-gap report.
