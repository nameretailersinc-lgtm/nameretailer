A few useful details.

Does this tool upload my text?

No. This standalone preview performs its counts in your browser and makes no network requests. Your text is not stored, and refreshing the page clears it.

Why can word counts differ between tools?

Tools use different rules for punctuation, hyphenated expressions and languages. This demo uses the browser’s word segmentation when available and states its fallback rule below the input.

How is reading time calculated?

The estimate divides words by 200 and rounds up to a whole minute for nonempty text. This is a configurable assumption, not a measured prediction of how quickly you will read.

What counts as a paragraph?

A nonempty block separated by one or more line breaks counts as a paragraph in this demo.
