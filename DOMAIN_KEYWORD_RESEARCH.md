# Domain keyword research — 2026-10-11

## Scope and decision

Research covers the owner's four proposed clusters for English-language domain buyers and business founders. No country was specified, so this is an initial English-language, international intent review. Search results were accessed through the available web search tool, not a controlled, location-specific Google rank tracker. The results establish useful page formats and competing destinations; they do not establish search volume, organic difficulty, traffic potential or fixed Google positions.

The inspected application currently supports **guest-post publication placements**, not buying ownership of domain names. `lib/commerce/types.ts` describes publication metrics, placement requirements, link type and turnaround. `lib/commerce/cart-types.ts` describes placement and writing selections. `lib/tools/catalog.ts` lists the working tools, and `app/api/tools/rating/route.ts` retrieves supplied publication scores. No domain-sale inventory, registrar availability integration, transfer workflow, domain generator or market-value appraisal implementation was found in the web application. A domain in the publication catalogue is a publisher address, not evidence that its registration is for sale.

**Decision:** retain the current marketplace's purpose. Prepare a small domain-content plan; publish domain sale, industry and tool pages only when their corresponding inventory or functionality exists. This research adds no public routes, generated articles, sitemap entries or changes to commerce APIs.

## A bounded first release

1. Validate audience demand and the site's intended domain offering before public implementation. Keep the current placement catalogue and its internal publication links accurate.
2. Commission at most the three distinct editorial candidates above first, if domain education fits the intended audience. Each needs original analysis or a usable worksheet, a real author/reviewer, checked sources and honest publication dates. This document is a brief, not three finished articles.
3. Build one domain-sale hub only after real inventory and fulfilment exist. Treat short names as a filter/section initially. Add the three industry collections only when each provides materially different inventory and buyer guidance.
4. Release tool pages individually after their functionality is demonstrated. The existing catalogue Domain Rating lookup remains a publication-score tool; it is not an availability checker or monetary appraisal.

Do not generate pages for every extension, length, price band, industry and adjective combination. Empty collections should not become indexable landing pages. Domain detail pages need a genuine listing and unique purchase-relevant information, rather than a template that merely substitutes the domain name. Future routes should have one canonical destination per intent, server-rendered useful content and direct internal links. Sitemap inclusion follows a real, usable page.

## Quantitative research to complete before publication

Use a keyword export scoped to the actual buyer market and language. Required columns: query, country, language, source, observation period, average monthly searches, monthly trend, intended page, current search-result format, existing Search Console impressions/clicks and inventory/tool readiness. Review the queries together to avoid double-counting close variants. Keep missing measurements empty rather than substituting estimates.

Google Ads Keyword Planner can provide search estimates with selected locations and date ranges [S13]. Its competition column concerns advertisers; it is not an organic ranking-difficulty measurement. Collect organic difficulty only from a named, dated provider, with its methodology distinguished from ad competition. Search Console supplies evidence of the site's own queries and pages [S14]. Neither source has been accessed through an authenticated account during this review.

Prioritize an intent the site can actually fulfil, then compare verified demand and the effort needed to supply something useful. A keyword phrase being present in search results does not prove high demand. The earlier owner's “1,062 keywords,” “796 gaps” and “Low” labels do not validate demand for these new domain clusters; their missing source/export remains documented in `KEYWORD_MAP.md`.
