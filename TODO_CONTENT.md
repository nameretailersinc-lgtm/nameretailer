# Owner content and verification

Do not place unresolved items or invented facts on public pages.

- Supply approved Terms of Service. The existing `/terms/` route links to real policies and contact information; it must not invent contractual terms.
- Confirm publisher-specific delivery, removal and replacement commitments. Preserve the existing owner-approved Refund, Privacy and Cookie policies; FAQ explanations do not introduce new guarantees.
- Supply verified team biographies, job titles, reviewer identities and original publication dates where absent. Existing names and dates may be shown only where source records/config support them.
- Confirm real official social profiles before adding Organization `sameAs`, and supply documented customer evidence before enabling any numeric trust claim.
- Consolidate the existing owner-confirmed legal/controller and contact-address facts into configuration. Confirm which address serves each purpose; do not invent registration identifiers or a founding date.
- Provide publication-specific editorial text for listings that lack enough unique content for indexing. Catalogue activation and supplied metrics are not editorial verification.
- Connect Search Console and GA4 using the existing environment hooks; verify indexing, sitemap discovery, consent behavior and events in those accounts.
- Validate any live gateway failure against deployment/reverse-proxy logs and check field Core Web Vitals after deployment. Local builds do not prove live health or organic traffic.
